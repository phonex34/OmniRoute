- **fix(combo):** An authenticated native Codex turn that completes normally with no visible output now reaches Claude-format clients as a successful empty `end_turn` (HTTP 200) instead of being rejected as a synthetic 502 and retried or failed over. Per Anthropic's stop-reason semantics an empty `end_turn` is a successful response, and clients such as watchdog agents that are instructed to stay silent rely on it; the client decides whether to continue. Unauthenticated, failed, incomplete, and content-filtered Codex completions still fail over.
